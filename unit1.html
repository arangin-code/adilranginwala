Blog: Gen Z and Vapes

The phenomenon I want to explore is why I feel that Generation Z uses vapes (electronic cigarettes) more than cigarettes. I selected this because it is something that I have observed in my everyday life. When I see older people smoking cigarettes, I feel they usually are because smokers are already used to smoking cigarettes rather than vaping. When I see Gen Z smoking cigarettes, they seem to be doing so in a more secretive way. Although, when I see Gen Z vaping they often will do so in front of other people and seem more willing to do so. It may be because it has a younger aspect to it that cigarettes don’t (for example brighter colors). My interpretation of those observations is that cigarettes have garnered a sort of stigma over time and vaping is seen as the “cooler” option. I think that the marketing of vapes and social media also add to this.

Some assumptions that I have are that advertising and social image play a huge role in why Gen Z vapes more than smoking cigarettes. So for my “So what,” “how,” and “why” questions I have, I want to ask: why is vaping more socially acceptable than cigarettes among Gen Z? Why did cigarettes acquire a negative stigma? How does advertising play a role in how Gen Z views vapes compared to cigarettes? For my three popular culture artifacts I will use a TikTok, a meme, and a vaping related video, with the TikTok being my strongest lens because a big way of how young people see trends or adopt social behaviors is through social media. Looking at the TikTok could broaden my interpretation because it isn't mainly advertisements that are causing vaping's favorability among Gen Z but I believe it is common people posting on social media which makes it seem more normal and hidden than advertisements that have an obvious agenda. While Gen Z favoring vapes over cigarettes could be because vapes are less harmful than cigarettes, by looking at the push of vaping in social media I believe the main reason is because of the different social image and stigma that surrounds cigarettes.
