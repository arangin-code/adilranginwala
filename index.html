ABOUT ME

A CASUAL INTRODUCTION TO WHO I AM

My name is Adil Ranginwala. I was born in Evanston Illinois (by Chicago) and raised in South Bend IN. I am a Neuroscience major on the pre-med track. I like traveling to new places and playing pickleball. I graduated high school a year early and took a gap year before starting college. In that gap year I travelled to Turkey, Malaysia, Singapore, and lived in Saudi Arabia for almost three months. I beleive that through travelling and witnessing different cultures, you can think from new perspectives that aid critical thinking. Also, I can speak Urdu (native language of Pakistan) and read Arabic. I am involved in the Muslim Student Association and the Pakistani Student Association as being a Muslim and Pakistani are both things that have a significant effect on who I am. I like to read books that I am interested in although I am not very fond of writing. My favorite book is the Autobiography of Malcolm X which sounded pretty boring when I first heard about it. However, it turned out to be a book I never forgot and one book I still reflect on from time to time even after having finished it. I can tolerate writing when it is something that I am passionate about. For example, I had a research paper in tenth grade where we could choose any topic to write about. I enjoyed that because I felt connected to what I was writing instead of simply trying to earn the points.
